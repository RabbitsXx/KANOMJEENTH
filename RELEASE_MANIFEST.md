## Launch gameplay decisions
Intentionally included:
- player-request TPA with PvP/raid/zone gates
- persistent homes with strict limits
- conservative utility kits
- short respawn protection
- build-count/restricted-zone protection
- objective-aware airdrops
- persistent stats
- safe save/restart/announcements
- moderation + local audit
- vehicle inspection/logging

Intentionally excluded at launch:
- public `/warp`
- virtual vault
- unrestricted item/vehicle shop
- full economy dependency
- public virtual garage
- VIP guns/end-game kits
- VIP combat/raid/deadzone bypass
- unlimited homes
- loot boxes/battlepass
